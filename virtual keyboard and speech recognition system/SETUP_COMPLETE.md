## 🔍 What Happens Behind the Scenes

```
Your Hand
    ↓
Webcam Captures Image
    ↓
MediaPipe Detects Hand Landmarks
    ↓
Software Recognizes Gestures (Pointing, Fist)
    ↓
Your Hand Position is Mapped to Keyboard
    ↓
Keys Highlight as You Point
    ↓
Fist Closes → Click Detected
    ↓
Text Added to Display
    ↓
You See Results Instantly
```
